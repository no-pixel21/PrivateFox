Turn the blocking up. Or down.

Private Fox is a Firefox-based browser with uBlock Origin built in and a single control for how strict it is. No telemetry, no sponsored tiles, nothing phoning home.

Download Private Fox All downloads

Free and open source. For Windows 10/11 (64-bit) and Macs with Apple silicon.

Five levels, one click

The shield in the toolbar shows your current level. Click it, pick another one, and Private Fox restarts with the new rules. If a site breaks, switch filtering off for just that site.

0Off

Nothing is blocked. Telemetry stays off anyway.

Breaks: nothing.

1Balanced

Ads and trackers gone, strict tracking protection, no link prefetching.

Breaks: almost never anything.

2Strong Default

Plus malware and phishing lists, encrypted DNS, your IP hidden in video calls, tracking parameters stripped from links.

Breaks: rarely, an odd embedded login.

3Extreme

Plus no cookie banners, HTTPS only, fingerprinting protection, camera and microphone blocked.

Breaks: some older or HTTP-only sites.

4Paranoid

Only the site you visit may load anything. WebGL and WebRTC off, cookies wiped on exit.

Breaks: many sites. That's the point.

What Private Fox never does

These are switched off at every level, including level 0.

  • Send telemetryUsage data, health reports and crash reports are locked off.
  • Run studies or experimentsNo remote feature tests on your browser.
  • Show sponsored contentNo sponsored shortcuts, stories or search suggestions.
  • Recommend add-ons or featuresNo pop-ups nudging you towards anything.
  • Ask for your location or notificationsThose prompts are blocked from level 1 up.
  • Track you on this websiteThis page loads nothing from other servers. No fonts, no analytics.

Questions

Is this just Firefox?

It is Firefox's engine and code, built from Mozilla's official source, with our own settings, policies and the level switch added on top. Private Fox is not made or endorsed by Mozilla.

Which ad blocker does it use?

uBlock Origin, the open-source blocker that works best in Firefox. Private Fox installs it for you and sets its filter lists according to your level. You can still open its settings and add your own filters.

How do updates work?

Private Fox doesn't update itself. A new build with Firefox's latest security fixes is published every Monday. Download it and install it over the old version. Your bookmarks and settings stay. On GitHub, “Watch → Custom → Releases” sends you a notification for each new version.

Why does my system warn me when I install it?

Code-signing certificates cost money, and Private Fox doesn't have one yet. The warning means the file isn't signed. It doesn't mean something is wrong with it. The full source and build process are public on GitHub.

Can I use it in my language?

Private Fox ships in English. Open about:addons, search for your language pack (for example “German language pack”), install it and pick the language under Settings.

Does Private Fox collect any data?

No. Telemetry and studies are locked off, and the browser has no account system of its own. Sites you visit can of course still see what any site sees. The levels decide how much of that gets through.

Is there a version for Intel Macs or Linux?

Not yet. Builds currently cover Windows (64-bit) and Macs with Apple silicon.
